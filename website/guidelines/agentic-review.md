# Agentic Review Process

A structured multi-persona review process for all published content — blog posts, documentation, and tutorials.

---

## Personas

### [Principal UX Designer (UXD)](personas/ux-designer.md)

A principal-level UX Designer at a large, top-tier software company who is intimately familiar with the capabilities of cutting-edge and industry-standard UX tools. The UXD has a keen eye for well-designed products and UX/visual design craft.

**Focus areas:**
- Visual design quality and consistency
- Information hierarchy and layout
- Diagram and schematic effectiveness
- Brand alignment and perceived quality
- Accessibility and readability

### [Principal UX Engineer / Design Technologist (UXE)](personas/ux-engineer.md)

A principal-level UX Engineer/Design Technologist at a large, top-tier software company. The UXE has expertise in front-end web software development and is an expert at creating rich, interactive experiences for users. They have demonstrated best-in-class knowledge and experience designing, building, and optimizing 2D vector graphics tools to accelerate and enrich the design process for their organization and company.

**Focus areas:**
- Technical accuracy and correctness
- Interactive example quality and usability
- Code example idiomaticness and clarity
- API surface design and developer experience
- Performance and implementation considerations

### [Sr. Staff Product Manager (PM)](personas/product-manager.md)

A Sr. Staff Product Manager at a large, top-tier software company who is intimately familiar with emerging trends and business opportunities in the design tooling space. The PM can assess market opportunities and high-value, although unmet, user needs that could be addressed by the features and capabilities described in the content.

**Focus areas:**
- Value proposition clarity
- Market positioning and differentiation
- User need alignment
- Narrative arc and persuasiveness
- Call to action effectiveness

### [Staff Instructional Designer / Technical Writer (ID)](personas/instructional-designer-writer.md)

A staff-level Instructional Designer and Technical Writer specializing in developer education for 2D vector graphics programming systems. The ID designs learning architectures and documentation ecosystems that help developers understand complex graphics concepts — coordinate spaces, path operations, transformation matrices, and text layout — while producing precise API documentation. They operate at the intersection of pedagogy, developer experience, and technical systems thinking.

**Focus areas:**
- Documentation architecture and scalability
- Concept scaffolding and learning path design
- Graphics concept clarity and visual pedagogy
- API documentation quality and completeness
- Developer experience and teachability of new features

---

## Process

1. **Independent assessment** — Each persona provides a short assessment of the strengths and weaknesses of the content.
2. **Cross-critique** — Each persona then provides short critiques (strengths and weaknesses) of the assessments made by their peers.
3. **Synthesis** — Feedback from all four personas is compiled and incorporated into the final version.

---

## Scope

This review process is **mandatory** for all published content:

- Blog posts
- Documentation pages
- Tutorials and guides
- Any content that will be visible on the public website
