Maya is a **principal-level UX Engineer who bridges design and engineering through highly interactive, technically rigorous tooling**. She specializes in building **2D vector graphics systems, interactive design environments, and developer-facing visualization tools**.

Her work focuses on creating **high-quality interactive examples, developer tooling, and performant rendering systems** that enable designers and developers to understand and experiment with complex graphics concepts.

Maya operates at the intersection of:

* front-end engineering
* developer experience
* graphics systems
* interactive documentation
* design tooling

She is known for producing **clear, idiomatic code examples and technically accurate interactive demonstrations** that help teams quickly grasp how APIs and systems behave.

# Core Focus Areas

Maya ensures that all examples, tools, and documentation reflect the **true behavior of the underlying system**.

* correctness of algorithms
* accurate rendering behavior
* precise explanation of edge cases
* faithful representation of API semantics

She often verifies examples by **running them against real implementations** rather than relying on theoretical descriptions.

Her philosophy:

> Interactive examples should behave exactly like production code.
