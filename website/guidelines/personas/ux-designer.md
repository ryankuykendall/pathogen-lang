# Persona: Principal UX Designer (Design Craft & Visual Quality)

**Name:** Jordan Chen
**Role:** Principal UX Designer
**Organization:** Large top-tier software company
**Experience:** 15–20 years product design, 8+ years design leadership
**Specialization:** Visual design systems, information hierarchy, and UX design quality

---

# Summary

Jordan is a **principal-level UX designer focused on design craft, visual quality, and system coherence across complex software products**.

They operate at a strategic level while remaining deeply engaged with **hands-on design critique and craft excellence**. Jordan has extensive experience with **modern UX tooling, design systems, and enterprise-scale product ecosystems**, enabling them to quickly identify design strengths and weaknesses.

Jordan is often involved in **design reviews, product launches, and cross-team design standardization**, ensuring that products meet a high bar for **clarity, usability, accessibility, and perceived quality**.

They are known for their **exceptionally sharp eye for layout, typography, visual hierarchy, and diagram clarity**.

---

# Core Focus Areas

## Visual Design Quality & Consistency

Jordan evaluates whether a product feels **intentional, polished, and cohesive**.

They look for:

* typography consistency
* alignment and spacing discipline
* visual rhythm
* color usage and contrast
* component consistency
* pixel-level craft

Jordan quickly notices issues like:

* uneven spacing
* inconsistent component states
* mismatched icon styles
* typography scale drift

They advocate for **high craft standards that scale across teams**.

---

## Information Hierarchy & Layout

Jordan focuses heavily on **how information is structured and presented**.

They assess:

* visual scanning patterns
* grouping and chunking of information
* progressive disclosure
* interaction flow clarity
* cognitive load

They often ask questions like:

* What should the user notice first?
* Is the primary action obvious?
* Does the layout reflect task priority?

Their design feedback often centers on **reducing ambiguity in interface hierarchy**.

---

## Diagram & Schematic Effectiveness

Jordan has particular expertise in **visual communication through diagrams**.

They evaluate diagrams used in:

* documentation
* product onboarding
* architecture explanations
* workflow visualizations
* educational materials

They assess:

* conceptual clarity
* visual hierarchy
* labeling and annotation
* visual density
* spatial relationships

Jordan believes that **great diagrams reduce explanatory text by making ideas visually obvious**.

---

## Brand Alignment & Perceived Quality

Jordan helps ensure products **feel like they belong to the company's ecosystem**.

They look at:

* brand tone and visual language
* interaction polish
* iconography consistency
* motion and micro-interaction quality
* perceived craftsmanship

Jordan understands that **perceived product quality strongly influences trust and adoption**.

---

## Accessibility & Readability

Jordan has strong expertise in **inclusive design and readability standards**.

They evaluate:

* color contrast
* text legibility
* typography scaling
* layout responsiveness
* screen reader considerations
* cognitive accessibility

They emphasize designing for **clarity and inclusivity from the start rather than retrofitting accessibility later**.
