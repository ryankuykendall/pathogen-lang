Detail hero viewer — three explorations

Elevating the hero on the public workspace detail page from a static raster plate to a mini-workspace-grade viewer: pan, zoom, fullscreen, and layers over the pre-compiled SVG. All three variants are live — drag, ⌘-scroll, and toggle layers in each. Artwork is the real experiments-in-random-variable-offsets workspace (485 layers).

Variant A · conservative

Refined plate

Keeps today's plate exactly — crop marks, elevation, cream card — but the art grows from 720px to 1040px and gains hover-revealed glass controls.

  • Least visual change; zero layout risk below the hero
  • Layers live in a compact popover
  • Viewer chrome invisible until you reach for it
Open variant A → Variant B · instrument

Framed viewer

The stage becomes a framed instrument: a slim toolbar (layers count, fit, fullscreen) over a full-width viewport. The art spans ~1216px. Reads as "this is a live plate."

  • Discoverable controls — nothing hidden behind hover
  • Layers slide in as a drawer; ⌥-click solos a layer
  • Fullscreen keeps the toolbar — most app-like
Open variant B → Variant B·2 · chosen direction

Frameless + full viewport

Variant B after design review: the toolbar is gone entirely — just the framed art with a hover-revealed fullscreen toggle. "Fullscreen" fills the browser viewport (a fixed overlay), and the layers toggle appears only in that mode.

  • Quietest possible resting state: one glass button
  • Viewport-fill instead of the Fullscreen API
  • Layers drawer (⌥-click solo) gated to full-viewport mode
Open variant B·2 → Variant C · dramatic

Full-bleed stage

The viewport IS the plate: edge-to-edge art on a soft gradient backdrop, glass controls floating in the corners, and a collapsible swatch-dot layers rail on the left.

  • Maximum art per pixel; tallest viewport
  • Signature move: the glass layers rail over the artwork
  • Crop-mark identity moves to the meta block below
