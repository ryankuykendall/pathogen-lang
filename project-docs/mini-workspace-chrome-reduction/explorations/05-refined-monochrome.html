Strategy: keep a single header but let the title be the star. Serif display at 18px with tight negative tracking carries the band. Icon cluster sits on a hairline baseline with ·-width dividers between action groups. Color pickers collapse into a tidy dropdown opened from a single button showing three color dots — preserves the at-a-glance chip preview without a dedicated row.

Typography: Playfair Display (serif title + caption), Figtree (UI), JetBrains Mono (code). Palette: warm off-white #faf7f2, graphite #1a1a1d, oxblood #7a2e2a used only for the accent link on hover and the minimap viewport rectangle.

Chrome total: ~56px header — 68% reduction vs baseline. Taller than E02/E03 on purpose: it pays the height back in title prominence and generous target sizes. Caption lives as an italic subtitle directly beneath the title. PATHOGEN watermark is a small-caps whisper at the bottom-right of the preview.
