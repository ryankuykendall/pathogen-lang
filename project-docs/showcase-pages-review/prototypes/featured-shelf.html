Pathogen of the week

Crown Bloom

M by @mira-aoki Featured May 12, 2026

Mira's twenty-four-petal bloom is the cleanest demonstration we've seen of PathBlocks composing with rotate at constant-angle intervals — every petal is one cubic bezier, the rest is loop and transform. There's nothing else in the file. It's the kind of economy we want everyone to find when they reach for Pathogen. — The editors, Pathogen Studio
