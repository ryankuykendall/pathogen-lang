Volume I  ·  Showcase № 014

Three works in the spotlight this week, each chosen for the way it takes a small idea and lets it carry the whole piece.

In the spotlight

Crown Bloom

M @mira-aoki May 12

A loop, a rotation, and one cubic bezier. Mira's bloom finds economy in places most of us would reach for ten primitives.

In the spotlight

Sine Repeater

L @leon-pak May 10

Math, plotted carefully, becomes composition. Leon's eight waves at phase-shifted offsets are about timing as much as shape.

In the spotlight

Lone Star, Plotted

S @sasha-tanaka May 9

A single primitive set against orbit guides and a half-tone field — context as composition, primitive as content.

Carrying over from last week

Polychrome Halo

R @ryan-kuykendall May 14

A piece worth returning to. Twelve gradient rings, one offset wedge, and an uneven angular tempo that makes the whole composition read like sound.
