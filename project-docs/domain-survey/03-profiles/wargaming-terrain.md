# Wargaming Terrain & MDF Kits

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 3 · Pain 3 · Fit 4 · GapCost 3 · Adopters 3 = **324** · Longlist A8

## Snapshot
Laser-cut MDF buildings and scenery are a thriving seller economy (kits *and*
cut-files) with flat-pack's exact mechanics — tab-slot walls, etched detail
layers — plus wargaming's own ecosystem of bases, movement trays, and tokens.

## Description
Terrain makers and small manufacturers (Brutal Cities, Uncertain Scenery, TJH
Models tier) producing 28–32 mm scale buildings, ruins, and scatter for
Warhammer/Kill Team/historical play, sold assembled, as kits, or as cut files
on Etsy. Adjacent staples: bases (ovals/rounds at standard sizes), movement
trays, acrylic tokens. Design in Illustrator/Fusion; heavy etch layers for
brick/panel detail.

## Problems Pathogen could address
A building kit is flat-pack + surface detail: tab-slot walls (material-
thickness math), etched brick/window patterns (procedural texture — noise and
Grid territory), and scale variants (28 mm vs 32 mm vs 15 mm re-issues are
manual redraws today). Base/tray generators are pure parameter tables
(standard sizes × magnet holes × slot counts). Tournament "competitive
layout" sets need footprint-consistent families.

## Commercial value
Active kit + cut-file market (Etsy MDF terrain files sell as digital goods);
tournament/event bulk orders; base-and-token accessories are high-volume
consumables. Sellers are exactly the parametric-minded wedge.

## Missing features
### Domain-specific [D]
- Tab-slot wall joints by material thickness (shared with A1 flat-pack)
- Procedural etch textures (brick courses, planking, panel lines) as fills
- Scale-variant regeneration (one building, three scales, thickness re-solved)
- Base/tray/token generators at gaming-standard sizes with magnet holes
### General [G]
- Physical units; sheet nesting; DXF/lightburn export profiles; modules
  (architectural detail libraries)

  re-solved; (3) the base-and-tray generator every club wants.

## Pathogen fit today
cut()/labels for tab joints, noise + Grid for procedural etch detail (a
genuine differentiator — competitors hand-draw brick), layers for cut/etch.
Same unit/nesting/export gates as flat-pack.

## Proposed validation project
A parametric ruined corner building: tab-slot walls from material thickness,
noise-varied brick etch, 28→32 mm regeneration — cut in MDF and assembled.
