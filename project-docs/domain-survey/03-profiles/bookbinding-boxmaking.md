# Bookbinding & Box Making

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 2 · Pain 3 · Fit 4 · GapCost 4 · Adopters 3 = **288** · Longlist A10

## Snapshot
Cases, clamshells, and slipcases are thickness-driven parametric templates —
every dimension derives from the text block plus board and cloth allowances —
computed by hand from formula sheets today.

## Description
Hand bookbinders and journal makers (a growing craft segment) building cased
books, clamshell boxes, drop-spine boxes, and portfolios. The community
trades template PDFs and formula sheets: board dimensions = text block +
squares; spine width from paper bulk; turn-in and joint allowances by cloth
weight. Tools: rulers and math, Etsy templates, spine-template guides popular
in the journaling community; digital file packs (Bookbinding Kit-style
bundles) sell in volume.

## Problems Pathogen could address
Every binding structure is a formula family the binder recomputes per book:
change the text block and the whole case, spine, and box cascade. A clamshell
is two trays + case with nested clearances (tray-in-tray fit) — precisely the
material-thickness math flat-pack needs, in board-and-cloth form. Cutting
templates for board shears or Cricut cardstock are direct SVG/PDF output.

## Commercial value
Template/formula-pack sales into the journaling boom; workshop education;
box-making for photographers/archives (portfolio boxes are a paid product).
Modest, steady.

## Missing features
### Domain-specific [D]
- Structure library: case, clamshell (tray clearances), drop-spine, slipcase
  as parametric modules from text-block dimensions + board/cloth thickness
- Turn-in/corner-miter conventions for covering material layouts
- Grain-direction annotations
### General [G]
- Physical units; modules (the structure library IS the domain); multi-page
  tiled PDF for large templates; number formatting

## Pathogen fit today
Pure 2D formula geometry — buildable today minus units and multi-page
templates; GapCost 4 (kit work). Labels can annotate fold/cut/score lines
with the papercraft conventions.

## Proposed validation project
A clamshell box generator: text-block W/H/D + board and cloth thickness in,
tray/case templates with clearances out, tiled 100%-scale PDF — built
physically to verify the nested fit.
