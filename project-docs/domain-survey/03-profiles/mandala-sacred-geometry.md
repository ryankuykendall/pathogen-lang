# Mandala & Sacred Geometry Art

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 3 · Pain 2 · Fit 5 · GapCost 4 · Adopters 2 = **240** · Longlist C5

## Snapshot
Radial symmetric pattern art with a large mindfulness-framed audience —
low workflow pain, but near-perfect Pathogen fit and a direct feeder into
three other profiled domains (coloring books, stencils, laser decor).

## Description
Artists drawing mandalas (dot mandalas, line mandalas, Zentangle-adjacent
work) for wall art, painted rocks, laser-cut decor, and coloring pages;
sacred-geometry designers producing flower-of-life/metatron motifs for
prints and jewelry. Sold as finished art, templates, stencils, and coloring
content. Tools: compass and pencil, Procreate radial-symmetry brushes,
Illustrator rotate-repeat.

## Problems Pathogen could address
Radial repetition with per-ring parameter progression is the entire craft's
geometry — rings of petals/dots whose counts, sizes, and palettes step
systematically. Procreate gives live symmetry but no parameters; Illustrator
gives precision but no exploration. Dot-mandala *templates* (guide circles +
dot position grids) and stencil derivatives are direct outputs. Low pain
(artists enjoy the process) — Pathogen serves the template/product side, not
the meditative drawing itself.

## Commercial value
Feeds sibling markets: coloring interiors (C2), stencil products (A3),
laser-cut wall decor (A6 adjacency), template/workshop sales. As a hub motif
domain, its value is multiplied through them.

## Missing features
### Domain-specific [D]
- Ring/petal progression helpers (count/size/rotation series per ring)
- Dot-mandala template kit (guide circles, dot diameter ladder)
- Symmetry-group utilities beyond simple rotation (reflection groups)
### General [G]
- Parameter sliders in playground (live symmetry exploration is the
  Procreate-competing experience); almost nothing else — GapCost 4

## User base
Large diffuse audience (mandala content is mass-scale on Instagram/
Pinterest; dot-mandala kits a steady craft market) · confidence **L,
unverified**. Practitioner-to-adopter conversion low; template *sellers* are
the wedge.
