# Vinyl Signage & Vehicle Decals

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 3 · Pain 2 · Fit 3 · GapCost 3 · Adopters 2 = **108** · Longlist A21

## Snapshot
Professional sign and wrap shops cut vinyl at scale — a large industry whose
2026 design mood ("clarity over clutter") favors exactly the bold parametric
geometry Pathogen produces, but whose workflow pain is moderate and
incumbent-served.

## Description
Sign shops, wrap installers, and decal sellers running vinyl plotters
(Roland/Graphtec/Summa) with Flexi/VersaWorks/Illustrator pipelines.
Artifacts: storefront lettering, vehicle graphics, regulatory/wayfinding
signage, decal sheets. 2026 aesthetic: simpler, bolder brand-first designs;
holographic/chrome films on the wrap side.

## Problems Pathogen could address
Weeding-friendly geometry (minimum gaps, no hairline slivers) is checked by
eye; panelization (splitting large graphics across vinyl widths with
overlaps) is manual; size-series production (same logo at 6", 12", 24" with
stroke weights re-tuned) is repetitive. ADA/wayfinding signage has codified
spacing/contrast rules — a compile-checkable spec.

## Commercial value
Industry is large but professionally tooled; realistic entry is the decal-
seller end (Etsy/small shop) and spec-compliant wayfinding templates, not
displacing Flexi. Lowest score on the longlist's A cluster — profiled for
completeness.
