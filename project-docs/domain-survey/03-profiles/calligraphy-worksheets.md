# Calligraphy & Lettering Worksheets

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 3 · Pain 2 · Fit 4 · GapCost 4 · Adopters 2 = **192** · Longlist C7

## Snapshot
Practice sheets are parametric by definition — guideline grids of x-heights,
slant angles, and nib ladders — sold in volume on Etsy and Teachers Pay
Teachers as printable + Procreate bundles.

## Description
Calligraphy teachers and worksheet sellers producing guideline sheets (slant
grids for Copperplate at 55°, brush-lettering baselines, x-height ratios per
script), tracing alphabets, and drills. Market channels: Etsy (10-alphabet
bundles, 60+ page packs), TPT, teacher blogs (The Postman's Knock tier).
Formats: print PDF + Procreate import.

## Problems Pathogen could address
Every script has a guideline geometry (ascender/x-height/descender ratios,
slant angle, nib-width ladders) that sellers rebuild per page size and per
script in Illustrator. A guideline generator — script parameters in, letter-
size-graded sheet sets out — automates the whole non-artistic half. Dot-grid,
arc-drill, and flourish-practice geometries are similarly parametric.

## Commercial value
Real but modest: worksheet packs at $5–15 with steady volume; teacher
licensing; the generator itself as a seller tool. Feeds the planner/printable
economy rails (E2).

## Missing features
### Domain-specific [D]
- Script guideline models (ratio + slant presets for major scripts)
- Nib-width ladder rendering; drill-stroke pattern kit
- Procreate-friendly export sizing conventions
### General [G]
- Multi-page PDF; physical units; CLI batch (size-graded sets)
