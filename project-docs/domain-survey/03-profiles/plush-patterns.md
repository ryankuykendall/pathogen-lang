# Plush & Fursuit Patterns

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 2 · Pain 4 · Fit 3 · GapCost 2 · Adopters 3 = **144** · Longlist B6

## Snapshot
Plushie designers turn 2D character art into fabric panel patterns — a
passionate niche where dedicated 3D→pattern tools (Plushify) already exist,
confirming demand for computed patterns, but where the 3D-unwrap core is
beyond Pathogen's 2D scope.

## Description
Indie plush designers (Etsy/DeviantArt/convention sellers) and fursuit makers
drafting darted, gusseted panels for sewn character goods. The 2026 market
runs on personalization (embroidered names, custom pet replicas, QR story
cards) with adult buyers demanding high finish. Pattern design is iterative
prototyping: draft → sew muslin → adjust.

## Problems Pathogen could address
The 2D share of the work: pattern grading (one design, three plush sizes),
seam allowances (the recurring offset() story), mirrored pairs, dart/gusset
notation conventions, layout onto fabric widths with nap-direction arrows.
The genuinely hard core — deriving panels from a 3D form — is out of scope,
which caps Fit at 3; Pathogen serves the designers who already draft flat.

## Commercial value
Pattern sales (Etsy/Gumroad, active), commission work, kit sellers. Niche
but devoted; fursuit commissions are high-ticket.

## Missing features
### Domain-specific [D]
- Dart/gusset notation kit (fold lines, match points, notches)
- Size grading for plush proportions (non-uniform scaling rules)
- Fabric layout with nap/stretch direction arrows
### General [G]
- Reliable curve offset() for seam allowances (the garment blocker again);
  physical units; multi-page tiled PDF

## User base
Est. 50–200k active makers/designers · proxy: plush-pattern Etsy depth,
fursuit community scale, DeviantArt tutorial reach · confidence **L,
unverified**.

## Community & current conversation (as of 2026-08-30)
- **Where they gather:** plush-maker Discords, DeviantArt tutorial culture,
  TikTok sewing content, Etsy/Gumroad shops, furry-convention maker dens.
- **Talking about right now:** personalization dominates 2026 (custom pet
  replicas, embroidered names); Plushify-style 3D-model→pattern tools
  emerging; designers advertise turning flat character sketches into
  multi-panel patterns as a paid skill. (leelinetoys.com, deviantart.com,
  makeshiftwings shop, tiktok)
- **Obsessed with:** minky-fabric handling, invisible closures, embroidery
  faces, prototype-then-perfect workflow.
- **Blog content angles:** (1) seam allowances that survive curved panels —
  when offset() is fixed, this is the demo; (2) a graded three-size pattern
  from one source; (3) fabric layout with nap arrows computed.

## Pathogen fit today
Flat drafting, mirroring, labels, exact-scale PDF work; curve-offset
reliability and the 3D ceiling hold it back (GapCost 2 counts the offset
blocker as engine work).

## Proposed validation project
A simple gusseted plush (bean-style) pattern: mirrored panels, notches,
allowances, graded S/M/L, tiled PDF — sewn once to verify the notation reads.
