# Wedding & Event Stationery

**Tier:** physical-output · **Rubric:** Pop 4 · Pain 2 · Fit 4 · GapCost 3 · Adopters 2 = **192** · Longlist C6

## Snapshot
Invitation suites are variant-heavy layout systems — and 2026's trends
(die-cut shapes, layered vellum, cohesive identity systems, even
puzzle-interactive invitations) push the market straight toward parametric
geometry and cutting machines.

## Description
Stationery designers and semi-pro sellers producing invitation suites:
save-the-dates, invites, RSVP cards, menus, seating charts, signage — one
visual identity across ~10 artifact types. 2026 trends: intricate die-cut
shapes (scallops, waves, ticket edges — cut-machine territory), heavyweight
stocks, vellum/acrylic layering, experimental type, envelope liners.
Tools: Canva/Illustrator templates, Cricut for die-cut edges, print
services (StationeryHQ tier).

## Problems Pathogen could address
The suite is one identity propagated across sizes and pieces — today by
hand-adjusting ten templates. Per-guest variants (names on place cards,
addressed envelopes, seating charts from a guest list) are mail-merge
geometry blocked only on data import. Die-cut edge shapes (scalloped/wavy
borders) are parametric curves; liner templates fold-fit specific envelope
sizes.

## Commercial value
Large semi-pro seller market (Etsy stationery is huge); per-event
customization is billable work that batch tooling makes profitable; template
businesses scale. Buyers non-technical — sellers are the audience.

## Project ideas (as of 2026-10-07)
Tiered by Pathogen skill and readiness. Web-researched; a dated snapshot.

### Beginner — buildable today
- **Belly band with monogram window** — A belly band wraps the invitation
  suite; a popular version has a heart or circle window framing a monogram or
  photo. It is a long strip with fold marks, a cut-out and centred type.
  Changing the window shape or initials regenerates the cut file, and the band
  length is a single value.
- **Table number cards** — Tent or flat cards numbered one to twenty, with a
  border and the couple's names in small type. Each card is the same layout
  with a different numeral, so a loop lays out the full set as print artwork
  with a cut line. It shows repetition with variation before any guest data is
  involved.

### Intermediate — several features, or one gap
- **Arch-top invitation with backing layers** — The arched invitation, mounted
  on a slightly larger coloured backing card and sometimes a vellum overlay,
  is a current favourite shape. The backing outline is the invitation outline
  grown by a fixed margin, and each paper is a layer. Type, die-cut curves,
  offsets and layers combine, and all three cuts come from one definition.
- **Menu, programme and signage set** — Day-of paper goods — menus, ceremony
  programmes, bar and welcome signs — share one layout system at several
  sizes. Borders and type styles defined once can be reused piece by piece.
  Sending the printer one file holding every piece is the obstacle: multi-page
  PDF is listed as a general gap, so each piece exports separately.
- **Addressed envelope set** — Guest addressing, with names and addresses set
  in the suite's typography and centred on each envelope, is a service
  stationers charge for per piece. The layout is straightforward text
  placement. Running it across a guest list needs data import, the gate the
  profile names; until then the list has to be typed into the program.

### Advanced — depends on a named gap
- **Scalloped favour tag** — Favour tags are an early project in wedding
  cutting-machine guides: a small card with a decorative edge, a punched hole
  and two lines of type. The scalloped outline should fit a whole number of
  scallops around the perimeter, corners included. That depends on the die-cut
  edge library listed as missing; until it exists the maker builds the scallop
  run by hand and re-tunes it for every tag size.
- **Seating chart board** — The large acrylic or mirror seating chart, with
  guests grouped by table in cut vinyl or print, is a centrepiece order and
  changes until the final week. Each revision means re-flowing columns by
  hand. It depends on the seating-chart generator listed as missing — tables
  and a guest list in, a balanced layout out — along with data import.
- **Pocket-fold invitation enclosure** — A pocket fold is a scored card jacket
  with an inner pocket holding the reply card and details, sized precisely to
  its envelope. Score lines, pocket depth and panel widths all follow from the
  envelope standard. It depends on the envelope-liner and fold templates by
  envelope standard listed as missing, which sellers currently keep as
  hand-drawn template files.
- **Boarding-pass save-the-date** — Destination-wedding save-the-dates styled
  as tickets or boarding passes use notched corners, perforation lines and a
  tear-off stub. Ticket edges are among the die-cut shapes in current trend
  lists. A reusable, adjustable version depends on the die-cut edge library
  the profile lists — scallop, wave and ticket borders as parametric frames.
